I'm a second-year doctoral researcher at the [Language, Society and Culture Studies Laboratory](https://lscs-lab.ma), [Hassan II University of Casablanca](https://www.univh2c.ma/en), working at the intersection of **computational linguistics, corpus analysis, and academic discourse in multilingual higher education**. Alongside my research, I founded [NLP4Ed](https://nlp4ed.com), a community bridging Natural Language Processing and English as a Foreign Language research.

### What I Work On
- **Corpus & Computational Linguistics:** Building and analyzing spoken and written corpora, focusing on lexico-grammatical patterns, dependency grammar, and cross-linguistic influence.
- **Applied Natural Language Processing & Text Analytics:** Investigating sentiment analysis, bias quantification in news discourse, automated linguistic profiling, and token-level code-switching.
- **Community & Open Science:** Leading [NLP4Ed](https://nlp4ed.com) and organizing academic workshops to make computational methods and open-source linguistic tools accessible to researchers and educators.
